Distilling Kumo-Tabular into HistGradientBoosting quantile models

Electricity load forecasting benchmark of probabl-ai/skore-example-electricity-load (walk-forward CV with forecast weather, 8 folds of 13 weeks, horizons 1h / 12h / 24h). The question: can a family of scikit-learn HistGradientBoostingRegressor models, distilled from Kumo-Tabular with the CutMix recipe of arXiv:2610.01435, close the gap between hgb_quantile_q7 and kumo_tabular? And does post-hoc calibration help on top?

Status: intermediate results. The runs listed under "In progress" are still training.

Iterations

  1. Reproduction of kumo_tabular and hgb_quantile_q7 on an AWS L4 GPU.
  2. Distillation into HGB students: the paper recipe, teacher diagnostics and single-fold probes.
  3. Post-hoc calibration from past out-of-fold predictions.

Findings so far

  • Both models are reproduced: hgb_quantile_q7 exactly (MAE 859.0 MW), and kumo_tabular within its ensemble's run-to-run noise (683 vs 688 MW published).
  • The paper's distillation recipe (squared-error students on teacher quantiles only, CutMix 2:1) closes only 7% of the MAE gap and 8% of the q7 pinball gap. It also makes the under-coverage worse (0.72).
  • The recipe assumes a teacher whose training-row predictions look like its test predictions, and queries on the data manifold. Neither holds here:
    • The teacher memorizes its in-context training rows (35-51 MW error, intervals 4 to 16 times narrower than on test rows).
    • CutMix of two random hours yields incoherent rows on which the teacher is 3 to 10 times wider than on test rows.
    • Blocked out-of-fold teacher predictions match the test statistics. They gave the best fold-0 probe, and a full run is in progress.
  • The largest gain so far does not come from distillation but from features. Lag differences and a residual target relative to the last observed load close 65% of the MAE gap (747 MW), most of it at 1h (320 vs 534 MW).
  • Calibration from past folds helps the under-covering HGB models when the median is left untouched: 90% coverage reaches 0.88-0.91 and the q7 pinball loss drops by 4-13 MW. Shifting the median always hurts, and calibrating the over-covering teacher does not help.
